How it works

Pick a project, build it in the open, and ship it to earn your passage.

Plan

Choose a project of any theme, from a first build to an advanced one.

Build

Write it as open source and track your hours with Hackatime.

Ship

Submit your finished project and collect groschen for the time you put in.

I. The Currency

Two coins circulate in the realm. Which one you earn depends on what you build.

S

Silver Groschen

The common currency, earned on general-themed projects. Ship open-source work and receive 10 silver groschen for every hour.

Spend at the General Store
G

Gold Groschen

The rarer coin, earned on medieval-themed projects. Submissions are expected to be high-quality and unique.

Spend at the Emporium

II. The Shops

Spend your groschen in one of two establishments.

The General Store

Pay in silver groschen
  • Stickers
  • Grants
  • Laptops and iPads
  • Other gadgets and goods

The Emporium

Pay in gold groschen
  • Special medieval-themed merchandise
  • Other high-quality prizes

Listed rewards are planned examples, not guaranteed stock.

III. The Rules of the Realm

  1. Every project must be open source.
  2. All work must be tracked with Hackatime.
  3. AI may be used only for debugging or very minor tasks.
  4. Projects from beginner to advanced can earn silver groschen.
  5. Gold groschen projects must be medieval-themed, high-quality and unique.

VI. The Questions

Answers to what travellers ask most.

What is Deliverance?

Deliverance is an online YSWS (You Ship Projects, We Ship Real Prizes) program with a 5 day exploration-hackathon. Work on open-source projects tracked with hackatime to earn groschen to purchase numerous rewards in the General Store and Emporium.

Is this free? Who can come?

Yep! Just ship open source projects to qualify and earn your event ticket!

Who's eligible?

If you're under 18 and still in high school.

Is my flight covered?

We'll cover travel for additional work submitted beyond the base hour requirement and hopefully provide need-based stipends as well.

Wait.. will we ACTUALLY get REAL armour?

If you want! We'll have grants you can spend on some medieval armour websites to get real medieval armour

I need more help!

Join #deliverance in the Hack Club slack and we'll help you! You'll get regular updates and notifications on Deliverance as well!
